# What's New in Futbolpedia

## Futbolpedia 3.6: debates about the past

- **Argued on clear criteria.** All-time debates and rankings say what the question turns on (peak level, longevity, output or influence), weigh each criterion, and answer the strongest case for the other side before committing to a verdict.
- **In line with the cards.** Verdicts and rankings are checked against the cards' ratings and peer placements. A question asked again in the same session gets the same verdict.
- **Facts from the record.** Past seasons and careers come from Wikipedia and ESPN's archives instead of the model's memory, and each fact stays in its own season. A club season or a player's best seasons is researched once and kept for everyone.
- **Figures that fit.** Answers quote only the figures that decide a point, each with its competition and seasons, so a league tally stays a league tally and a spell is never passed off as a career total.

## Futbolpedia 3.5: answers about right now

- **Today's team, not last season's.** Questions about a club start from who has been playing and who is out, read from live match data and the Fantasy Premier League feed, so answers name the current starters.
- **What changed, and why.** Answers lead with what's different this season, such as arrivals, departures, a new shape or new roles.
- **One starter per role.** The analyst commits to a first-choice player for each position and mentions rotation only where a team really rotates.
- **Fewer invented facts.** Figures nobody looked up are left out, and older articles that describe last season's team are set aside.
- **Quicker, consistent club facts.** Each club's current picture is worked out once a day and shared, so repeat questions are faster and everyone gets the same facts.

## Juggle multiple players and chats at once

This is the big one — everything below is built on top of it.

- **Multiple dossiers side by side.** Build a whole library of player dossiers and flip between them, instead of being stuck with one at a time. Compare, revisit, and keep your shortlist right at hand.
- **Multiple conversations at once.** Keep several scouting threads going in parallel and switch between them freely, so different players and questions each get their own space.

## Share your scouting

- **Send a dossier to anyone.** Every player dossier now has its own shareable link — one click copies it to your clipboard, ready to drop in a chat or group.
- **Share whole conversations.** Share an entire back-and-forth, dossiers included. When a friend opens your link, the full conversation lands in their library so they can pick up right where you left off.
- **Quick share buttons everywhere.** Hover over any dossier or past conversation and a link icon is right there — save and copy in one tap.

## Keep every chat

- **Conversation history.** Your chats are saved automatically, so you can jump back into any past conversation instead of starting over.
- **Cleaner, calmer interface.** Answers now stream in with a smooth typewriter effect and a refreshed loading indicator, and dossier controls moved up to the header so the chat stays focused on the conversation.
- **Dossier library, front and center.** The dossier button and its count are always visible, so your saved players are never more than a click away. Player age now sits right alongside position, club, and height in the header.

## Smarter, faster answers

- **Noticeably faster dossiers.** Popular players come back instantly, and even fresh scouting reports are generated meaningfully quicker than before.
- **Deeper quick answers.** Fast responses now dig into more sources behind the scenes, so you get richer, more complete scouting without the wait.
- **More accurate scouting reports.** Ratings now reflect a realistic spread of a player's real ability, and write-ups read like a genuine scout's take on a player's identity and role — not a dry list of stats.
- **Cleaner, more consistent dossiers.** Bios, position labels (like CDM/CAM), and tactical summaries are more polished and consistent, with no stray formatting slipping into your answers.
