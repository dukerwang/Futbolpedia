# What's New in Futbolpedia

Futbolpedia is your AI scout for every era of football. Since Futbolpedia 2, it has grown from a dossier generator into a shared scouting library with one rating scale for every era, an analyst that knows this weekend's lineup, and one that can settle the oldest arguments in the game.

## Futbolpedia 3.6: settle any debate

- **Greatest-ever questions, answered properly.** Who was better at their peak? Who's the best ever in a position? Get a clear verdict, argued point by point, that takes the other side's best case seriously.
- **Grounded in the record.** Past seasons and careers are checked against the historical record, so every fact is right and sits in the right season.
- **Consistent with the ratings.** Verdicts follow the library's ratings, so your argument and your cards tell the same story.
- **Stats you can quote.** Numbers come with their competition and seasons attached, ready for your next argument.

## Futbolpedia 3.5: football as it is today

- **This weekend's team.** Ask about any club and get the players who are actually starting and who's injured, not last season's lineup.
- **What's new, first.** Answers lead with what has changed: the signings, the new shape, the player who has taken over a role.
- **A real opinion.** The analyst picks one starter for every position and takes a clear stance, the way a scout would.
- **Facts it can back up.** Answers are built on live match data, and anything that can't be checked is left out instead of made up.
- **Faster for everyone.** Each club's current picture is worked out once a day and shared, so popular questions come back quicker.

## Futbolpedia 3: the Global Dossier Library

The biggest change since Futbolpedia 2. Dossiers stopped being private files and became one shared library for the whole community.

- **Scouted once, shared with everyone.** Every dossier anyone creates joins one library. You never start from a blank page, and the library grows with every player scouted.
- **Instant cards.** Look up a player who's already in the library and the card opens in under a second, whatever name you type.
- **Everyone argues about the same numbers.** There's one card for each version of a player, so your rating is everyone's rating. When enough people make the same good case against a card, it gets re-rated.
- **Explore the whole game.** Browse every card, filter by position, rating and edition, find the biggest risers, and jump from a player's current form to their prime, eras and single seasons.
- **One scale for every era.** A 1970s legend and today's breakout star are rated on the same all-time scale, and each card shows who a player was better than, level with and behind.
- **Every version of a player.** Rate current form, a prime, a named era such as a spell at one club, or a single season. Current-form cards are re-rated every month and refreshed every week.
- **Scout at speed.** Open Scout with ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows), paste a list of names, and keep chatting while they're rated in the background.
- **An analyst who knows your cards.** Type @ to bring any card into the chat, challenge a rating and keep your revised card if you win the argument, and ask about tactics, club form, rankings or transfers.
- **Gaffa mode.** Connect your fantasy league club for lineup audits, scored trade verdicts and bid ceilings drawn from your league's own rules.

## Futbolpedia 2

### Juggle multiple players and chats at once

This is the big one — everything below is built on top of it.

- **Multiple dossiers side by side.** Build a whole library of player dossiers and flip between them, instead of being stuck with one at a time. Compare, revisit, and keep your shortlist right at hand.
- **Multiple conversations at once.** Keep several scouting threads going in parallel and switch between them freely, so different players and questions each get their own space.

### Share your scouting

- **Send a dossier to anyone.** Every player dossier now has its own shareable link — one click copies it to your clipboard, ready to drop in a chat or group.
- **Share whole conversations.** Share an entire back-and-forth, dossiers included. When a friend opens your link, the full conversation lands in their library so they can pick up right where you left off.
- **Quick share buttons everywhere.** Hover over any dossier or past conversation and a link icon is right there — save and copy in one tap.

### Keep every chat

- **Conversation history.** Your chats are saved automatically, so you can jump back into any past conversation instead of starting over.
- **Cleaner, calmer interface.** Answers now stream in with a smooth typewriter effect and a refreshed loading indicator, and dossier controls moved up to the header so the chat stays focused on the conversation.
- **Dossier library, front and center.** The dossier button and its count are always visible, so your saved players are never more than a click away. Player age now sits right alongside position, club, and height in the header.

### Smarter, faster answers

- **Noticeably faster dossiers.** Popular players come back instantly, and even fresh scouting reports are generated meaningfully quicker than before.
- **Deeper quick answers.** Fast responses now dig into more sources behind the scenes, so you get richer, more complete scouting without the wait.
- **More accurate scouting reports.** Ratings now reflect a realistic spread of a player's real ability, and write-ups read like a genuine scout's take on a player's identity and role — not a dry list of stats.
- **Cleaner, more consistent dossiers.** Bios, position labels (like CDM/CAM), and tactical summaries are more polished and consistent, with no stray formatting slipping into your answers.
